-+ PQDIFNet 1.4.0 Release Notes + -
2022 July 12

	- Added methods to support setting the tagChannelGroupID value for channels. This is required to support Harmonic 
	and Interharmonic Group data.

	- Corrected the description of the PQDIF time stamp to reflect the actual base of Excel time stamps of 12/30/1899.

	- Added additional comments to clarify that PQDIF time stamps are UTC based.

	- New Tags: Added tagCharactMinVoltagePU, tagCharactMaxVoltagePU, tagCharactMinVoltage, tagCharactMaxVoltage, 
	tagCharactMinCurrent, and tagCharactMaxCurrent for min/max rms voltage and current

	- New Tag: Added tagSagTypeID for IEEE 1668 sag type

	- New IDs: Added equipment IDs.

	- New IDs: Added ID_QC_PRESSURE and ID_QC_PRESSURE_ATMOSPHERIC for storing pressure

	- New IDs: Added ID_SAGTYPE_1668_I, ID_SAGTYPE_1668_II, and ID_SAGTYPE_1668_III for 
	specifying IEEE Std 1668 sag type.

	- Fixed Misspelling in ID: Changed ID_QU_CELCIUS to ID_QU_CELSIUS.

Introduction

    The assembly will build as PQDIFNet.dll.
